## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to define an emergency and appropriate response.

## Scope

The scope of this policy includes any unplanned or undesirable change to the security status quo of [[SHORT_NAME]] information systems.

## Policy

The bank will use a response and resiliency strategy and plans designed to facilitate business continuity that can resist and recover from natural, man-made, and cyber related events and threats.

Examples include:

- Operating system crash on host systems or user equipment.

- Virus problems on host systems or user equipment.

- Cybersecurity events or an attack on the information system by a malicious person (insider or outsider).

- Inoperability of office automation equipment (i.e. fax, copiers, printers, telephone).

- Internet connectivity and/or e-mail not functioning.

- Vital applications not functioning (i.e. core processor, loan processor, check imaging application, alarm and video systems, etc.).

In the event there is an unwanted change in the information system security status quo, which results in a key resource becoming unavailable, the [[COMMITTEE]] shall be notified immediately.

## Compliance

The bank will use the following plans to implement this policy; Incident Response Policy & Plan, the Disaster Recovery Policy & Plan, and/or the Business Continuity Policy & Plan. The IT department will review, test, and revise these policies and plans to ensure the aforementioned policies and plans can adequately execute this policy.
