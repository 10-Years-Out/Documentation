## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify the security controls in place at the perimeter of the network to reduce the risk of attackers or automated malicious activity from entering the network.

## Scope

The scope of this policy encompasses all perimeter security devices and monitoring systems on the network (firewalls, IDS/IPS systems, etc.).

## Policy

### Firewall

- A physical [[FIREWALL_VENDOR]] firewall shall be deployed for the perimeter of the network at each internet and/or externally facing connection

- The firewall shall be configured with various security layers to protect the network from external attacks including, but not limited to, the following:
  - Intrusion prevention
  - Anti-virus and Anti-spyware
  - Content Filtering
  - Deep packet inspection
  - Botnet Filtering
  - Application Control
  - [[FIREWALL_ATP_SERVICE]]
  - SSL/Cipher Control
  - Details on firewall configuration and security settings can be found in the Information Security Program Documentation (ISPD).

- Along with the security layers mentioned above, the firewall shall be configured to only allow open ports for traffic that is necessary to perform normal business functions.

- The IT department shall be responsible for managing and monitoring the perimeter firewall.

### IDS/IPS

- Perimeter-based IDS/IPS shall be implemented for the network and utilize the perimeter firewall.
  - These devices shall be configured to monitor for and detect potential security incidents
  - The perimeter firewall shall be configured to prevent potential security issues by blocking malicious traffic before it is able to enter the network

- The IT department shall be responsible for managing the IDS/IPS solution including, but not limited to, performing the following tasks:
  - Reviewing logs and alerts within the console on a regular basis to determine if a pattern of malicious activity may be occurring or has occurred
  - Reviewing and responding to alerts generated by the system according to procedures outlined in the Incident Response Policy & Plan and the Information Security Program Documentation (ISPD)
  - Periodically performing a review of the configured perimeter firewall rules and settings to ensure only necessary traffic is allowed and all security services are active

- All of the systems used as part of the IDS/IPS solution shall have rules configured to detect and alert on anomalous activity according to the configuration documentation in the ISPD.

### Patch Management & End-of-Life Management for Perimeter Devices

- See the Patch Management Policy for details on patch management processes and procedures.

- See the End of Life Management Policy for details on EOL processes and procedures.

## Compliance

The IT department will oversee the internal monitoring and review processes, including review reports, for all perimeter security systems on the network. These reports will be presented to the [[COMMITTEE]] identifying all low, medium and high risks relating to perimeter security. Upon approval from the [[COMMITTEE]], the IT department will minimize these risks through a combination of shutting down unused services, closing ports, applying approved patches, updating software, and any other options available to secure the systems.
