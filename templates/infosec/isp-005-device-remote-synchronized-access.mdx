## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify the safeguards in place to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of [[SHORT_NAME]]'s information system as it regards mobile phone synchronization and remote access to bank email.

## Scope

This policy applies to remote and/or synchronized access to hardware, software, and storage media.

## Policy

Productivity tools such as synchronized mobile phones whether bank or employee owned and remote access to email must be managed and properly secured with the appropriate measures. The [[COMMITTEE]] will decide on a case by case basis which roles or employee's productivity and value to the bank will be increased by granting additional access. The bank may grant email access by bank or employee owned remote computer and remote email synchronization by either bank or employee owned mobile device.

All synchronized mobile devices must be approved by the [[COMMITTEE]] and synchronized by the IT department. Any support related to the synchronization must be performed by the IT department. Upon termination of an employee's employment, management must immediately notify the IT department to revoke the employee's access privileges.

All employees granted either remote or synchronized access must read and sign an agreement to abide by the Acceptable Use Policies.

### Security Measures for Remote or Synchronized Access

- In an emergency, the bank will enable and use [[WEBMAIL_PLATFORM]] to allow remote email access
- The [[WEBMAIL_PLATFORM]] is secured with an SSL certificate on an https connection using [[ENCRYPTION_KEY_LENGTH]] bit encryption
- Access will require the username and password employees use to access the bank's network
- Employees will comply with the following behavior:
  - Use only a secure computer in a secure location
  - Select the public computer setting when logging in
  - Log off when finished
  - Close the browser after logging off
  - When viewing attachments, do not save confidential bank or customer information to any device or storage media not approved for such use by the bank
  - Immediately report any suspicious activity or suspected loss of device control to the IT department.
  - Comply with all other policies of the bank.

### Security Measures for Synchronized Access

- The device will comply with the MDM system.
- The device access is secured with a pin number required by the MDM solution.
- Email will only be available through the MDM solution which will only store [[MDM_EMAIL_RETENTION]] worth of email.
- In the event of the loss of physical control or non-compliance of the device, the bank has the ability to wipe remotely the synchronized bank data and or all data from the phone or tablet.
- Employees will comply with the following behavior:
  - Use only an approved device setup by the IT department.
  - Ensure secure control and know the whereabouts of the device at all times.
  - Use a secure PIN and not share it with anyone.
  - Users must not store confidential information to device internal memory or memory card.
  - When viewing attachments, the device will store attachments in a secure containerized storage application.
  - Immediately report any suspicious activity or suspected loss of device control to executive management and the IT department.
  - Comply with all other policies of the bank.

### Privacy and Monitoring

Employees should have no expectation of privacy in the use of remote or synchronized services or bank owned devices. The services and or devices are subject to monitoring at any time and without notification.
