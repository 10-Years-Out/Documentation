### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to empower and ensure the bank's efforts to detect network performance and security events in order to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of [[SHORT_NAME]]'s information systems.

### Scope

This policy applies to all software and/or hardware existing on the [[SHORT_NAME]] network and the roles involved in monitoring the system.

### Policy

[[LEGAL_NAME]] shall employ various monitoring and/or alerting tools to detect, correlate, and review security incidents and/or events across the environment. The bank may accomplish this through either in-house resources or through a third party service. Tools, software, and hardware may include the following

- Configurable alerts and reports that are native to existing software and hardware
- Software and/or hardware designed and implemented specifically to detect and alert on network performance and security events.

#### Current Configuration

- For an overview of monitoring capabilities and guidelines, see the System and Network Monitoring document.
- For specific software and/or hardware alerting configurations, see the ISPD.

### Compliance

- The IT department is responsible for implementing monitoring capabilities and reviewing alerts and reports.
- If warranted the results of the reports and investigation will be reported to the [[COMMITTEE]] to determine a further course of action.
