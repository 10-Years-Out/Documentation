## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that information and cybersecurity program policies are updated.

## Scope

The policy applies to all information and cybersecurity program policies.

## Policy

- [[SHORT_NAME]] will update the information and cybersecurity program policies periodically as the cybersecurity threat landscape changes, new information and or technology becomes available.

- [[SHORT_NAME]] will log significant changes to the policies.

## Compliance

The changes will be reviewed and accepted by the [[COMMITTEE]].
