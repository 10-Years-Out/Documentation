### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish guidelines to ensure all personnel are aware of confidentiality and acceptable use.

### Scope

This policy applies to anyone coming in contact with any [[SHORT_NAME]] information systems and data.

### Policy

- Prospective Employees

  - Prospective employees may be subject to background and reference checks.

- Protective Measures

  - Users must protect confidential information within sight lines on their desks or working areas when unauthorized persons are in the immediate area.

  - Users must enable a screen saver, log-off, or take similar actions if unauthorized persons are in a position to see the screen of their microcomputers or computer terminals.

  - All Bank microcomputers storing confidential information have an installed and enabled password-based access control package.

- Secure Containers and Encryption

  - When not in use, or when not under direct and continuous visual supervision, confidential information must be stored in a secure manner.

- Reproduction

  - Reproduction of confidential information for non [[SHORT_NAME]] business use, including printing additional copies via a computer printer, is not permitted unless authorized by its owner. Likewise, extracts, summaries, translations, or derivatives of confidential information may not be made unless the information owner has previously approved it.

- Transport

  - Physical transport of confidential information by non-employees requires the use of an approved courier. All confidential information sent via couriers must be enclosed in a secure device.

- All employees are trained on the appropriate handling of information through the following policies

  - Privacy Policy
  - Personnel Policy
  - Acceptable Use Policy

### Compliance

All employees are required to read and agree to the above stated materials. The [[COMMITTEE]] will facilitate periodic training. An appointee or designee shall track the participation and results of the Information and cybersecurity training.
