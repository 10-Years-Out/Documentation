### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to empower the [[COMMITTEE]] to ensure the bank's efforts to manage and report capacity and/or performance in order to protect the availability of [[SHORT_NAME]]'s information systems.

### Scope

This policy applies to all servers existing on the [[SHORT_NAME]] network and the roles involved in monitoring and managing the system.

### Policy

The [[COMMITTEE]] shall direct IT resources to collect information and report capacity/performance for servers. The [[COMMITTEE]] may accomplish this through either in-house resources or through a third party service. Tools, software, and hardware may include the following:

- Configurable alerts and reports that are native to existing software and hardware.

- Software and/or hardware designed and implemented specifically to detect and alert on network performance.

- The frequency of reporting shall be no less than [[CAPACITY_REPORTING_INTERVAL_DAYS]] days, which may be accomplished through the bank's proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.

- The reports will be reviewed in summary by the [[COMMITTEE]] during [[COMMITTEE]] meetings.

- The [[COMMITTEE]] will provide summary reports to the BOD periodically.

#### Capacity Planning and Management

- The [[COMMITTEE]] shall consider server capacity/performance in the creation of all long-term strategic plans and annual project plans.
  - The [[COMMITTEE]] will consider how to efficiently retire and remove data.
  - The [[COMMITTEE]] will project future data storage needs and server capacity requirements and use the projections in planning.
  - The bank will consider how new services, systems, customers and employees will affect data storage needs and overhead on servers.

- If capacity/performance concerns arise during a project plan year, the situation will be evaluated and added to the current year's project plan if necessary.

- The following resources shall be available to allocate to servers at any given time in the virtual environment.
  - At least [[CAPACITY_MIN_FREE_DISK]] of free, available disk space for servers.
  - At least [[CAPACITY_MIN_FREE_CPU]] of free, available CPU allocation for servers.
  - At least [[CAPACITY_MIN_FREE_RAM]] of free, available RAM allocation for servers.

#### Reporting Expectations and Current Configurations

- For an overview of monitoring/reporting capabilities and guidelines, see the System and Network Monitoring document.

- The following thresholds shall be set for server resources and alerts generated if the thresholds are crossed
  - Any server CPU usage that exceeds [[CPU_ALERT_THRESHOLD]] for [[CPU_ALERT_DURATION_MINUTES]] minutes.
  - Any server RAM usage that exceeds [[RAM_ALERT_THRESHOLD]] for [[RAM_ALERT_DURATION_MINUTES]] minutes.
  - Any server disk drive the falls below [[DISK_ALERT_THRESHOLD]] of free, available disk space

- For specific performance/capacity configurations, see the ISPD.

### Compliance

- The [[COMMITTEE]] is responsible for overseeing the implementation of reporting/monitoring capabilities and reviewing reports to determine if capacity is appropriately managed. Reports will be reviewed through existing normal processes such as [[COMMITTEE]] meetings, and the bank's proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.

- If warranted the by results of the reports, the [[COMMITTEE]] shall direct IT resources to investigate and remediate any concerning issues through the bank's proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.
