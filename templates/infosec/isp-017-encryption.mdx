### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify safeguards in place to protect electronic information from disclosure.

### Scope

This policy applies to any electronically transmitted data traveling across public networks, including but not limited to, VPN connections, routers, and modems.

### Policy

- Any communication with the firewall from outside [[SHORT_NAME]] network shall be encrypted, either by the use of a VPN (Virtual Private Network) tunnel [[VPN_ENCRYPTION]] or higher, or the use of SSH (Secure Shell).

- Any web services hosting confidential information shall require secure authentication and encryption using [[WEB_ENCRYPTION]] TLS (Transport Layer Security) [[TLS_MINIMUM_VERSION]] or higher.

- All confidential, proprietary, or privileged information that is encrypted on a host system (server or workstation) should utilize a key length of [[ENCRYPTION_KEY_LENGTH]] bits.

- Approved removable storage devices should be issued by the bank and employ encryption.

- A specific list of encryption methods used will be kept in another technical document.

- All confidential data at rest on removable media shall be encrypted using AES-256 with a bank-managed key.

### Compliance

The [[COMMITTEE]] shall verify that all systems are implemented according to this policy. This policy will be updated as encryption standards evolve.
