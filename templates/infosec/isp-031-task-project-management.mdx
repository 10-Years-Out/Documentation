## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish a task and project management program for relevant IT system projects.

## Scope

The scope of the IT task and project management policy will cover all IT related tasks.

## General

The bank categorizes IT related tasks as Level 1 or Level 2 tasks. A project management process shall be utilized for Level 1 tasks as defined below. The IT department will be responsible for the monitoring of this process and reporting to the [[COMMITTEE_FULL]] ([[COMMITTEE]]).

## Definitions

- Level 1 tasks definition – Tasks that have an open-ended budget and/or will last more than [[LEVEL_1_TASK_THRESHOLD_DAYS]] business days.
- Level 2 tasks definition – Encompasses all tasks that do not rise to a Level 1.

## Level 1 and 2 Task Treatment

### Approval and Tracking

- Most planned tasks shall be accounted for, approved, and tracked by the annual IT controls budgeting process.
- Tasks shall be managed and tracked by the IT department and through the [[COMMITTEE]] reporting process.
- New tasks will be discussed, reviewed, and approved through the [[COMMITTEE]]. Following discussion and approval, the IT department shall add those tasks to the IT controls budget.
  - New tasks shall be evaluated based upon their practicality, priority, and application to the business objectives of the bank.
- The IT controls budget shall be reviewed quarterly by the [[COMMITTEE]].

## Level 1 Task Treatment

- The bank considers Level 1 tasks a project, and the bank shall utilize a specific project management process for Level 1 tasks due to the possibility of budget changes and the expected length of the task. The IT department shall be responsible for the management of the project and documenting the process in the appropriate documentation. The process will consist of documentation that communicates the following:
  - Projected/expended hours
  - Resource allocation
  - Start & End Dates
  - Project purpose/deliverables
  - Project approval documentation
  - Project status reports

- The documentation will be shared with appropriate bank staff and included in [[COMMITTEE]] and BOD meetings. The [[COMMITTEE]] shall be responsible for monitoring the process to ensure the process is properly documented and that the project is completed by its deadline.

## Level 2 Task Treatment

- Most tasks will be considered level 2 tasks and shall not be subject to the full project management process.
- Level 2 tasks will be accounted for, approved, and tracked by the annual IT controls budgeting process and through the quarterly [[COMMITTEE]] meetings.

## Testing

- On a task-by-task basis, the IT department shall determine the testing requirements for the task. The IT department shall oversee testing and test changes to determine that the changes had the desired results.
  - If the change did not achieve the desired results, the IT department shall report on and remediate the issues.

## Compliance

The [[COMMITTEE]] will ensure that the controls outlined in this policy are evaluated and reviewed according to the Auditing Policy.
