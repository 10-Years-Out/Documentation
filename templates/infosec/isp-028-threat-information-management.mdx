## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to draw attention to the changing threat landscape to cybersecurity and to foster the use and participation in threat information sharing as a tool to help manage cybersecurity risk.

## Scope

The policy applies to all personnel, information systems and electronic data.

## Policy

- All members of the [[COMMITTEE]] shall be enrolled in the [[THREAT_SHARING_FORUM]] threat information sharing forum and any other threat information sharing forums as deemed necessary.

  - All members shall be part of a mailing distribution group that receives all applicable threat intelligence information reports and alerts from [[THREAT_SHARING_FORUM]] or other applicable threat information sharing forums.

  - The applicability and associated risk of these threats to the institution is based on the institution's inherent risk profile and its administrative and technical controls in place.

  - When applicable, the [[COMMITTEE]] shall distribute threat information materials and provide the appropriate education to all employees in the institution.

  - Relevant threat information shall be made readily available to all customers on the bank's website when applicable.

- The [[COMMITTEE]] shall discuss all threat information received during its regular meetings

  - This information shall be compiled into a formal report that will be submitted for review and approval to the board.

- The bank will evaluate and adjust its membership volume and participation levels at the discretion of the [[COMMITTEE]].

## Compliance

Senior management should oversee the progress of this initiative and subsequent program by confirming the occurrence or existence of memberships, participation and information dissemination.
