## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to describe the bank's current, official use of social media and the manner in which it will manage future or additional use.

## Scope

The policy applies to all social media websites, services and applications.

## Policy

The platform lists, page ownership claims, administrator roles, and risk rating below are statements of the client's actual social media posture. Populate them from the client's environment.

- The [[COMMITTEE]] will discuss:

  - The risks and benefits of engaging in social media websites and applications prior to using social media.
    - Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Reputation, Compliance

  - Methods to control and mitigate risk
    - Scope of use
    - Access – employee and public
    - Administration
    - Monitoring – bank and public postings
      - Responding

- With the exception of [[SOCIAL_MEDIA_APPROVED_PLATFORMS]], [[SHORT_NAME]] does not participate in social media. At this time that includes but is not limited to:

  - [[SOCIAL_MEDIA_EXCLUDED_PLATFORMS]]

- [[SHORT_NAME]] claims ownership of its auto-generated [[SOCIAL_MEDIA_OWNED_PAGES]] pages.

  - To insure accuracy of phone numbers, hours and addresses.

  - To inform the community about events and events in which the bank participates.
    - [[SOCIAL_MEDIA_RESTRICTED_PLATFORMS]] will not be used in this manner.
    - [[SHORT_NAME]] will not provide detail on products and services.
    - [[SHORT_NAME]] will monitor posts from the public.

  - [[SHORT_NAME]] will use social media site settings to restrict administration to only approved employees.
    - [[SOCIAL_MEDIA_ADMIN_ROLES]]

  - [[SHORT_NAME]] will occasionally monitor the pages for compliance and consumer information requests.
    - Responses to posts from the public will immediately direct the recipient to formal channels of communication such as bank email and phone to resolve problems and complaints.

  - There is no other sanctioned use of social media at this time unless approved by the [[COMMITTEE]].
  - The risk of using social media in this limited manner is [[SOCIAL_MEDIA_RISK_RATING]].

- Employees are advised on their appropriate use of social media in the Acceptable Use Policy.

  - There are no sanctioned uses of personal social media sites for bank business unless previously approved by the [[COMMITTEE]].

  - The employees may maintain their own pages but should not engage in bank related business unless approved by the [[COMMITTEE]].

- Formal Marketing Initiatives

  - [[COMMITTEE]] Responsibilities
    - Review popular social media (including e-mail) and determine which can be utilized in a cost effective manner in the banks marketing strategy.
    - Develop a strategy to use the social media selected.
    - Communicate that strategy to all departments of the bank that advertise or market to customers.
    - Handling employee requests to market using social media.
      - Review employee requests to use social media.
      - Determine if it is in keeping with the bank's marketing strategy.
      - If it is not, work with the employee to reform it to comply.

  - All communications whether developed by employees or by management shall be provided to the [[COMMITTEE]] for approval.

    - After approval by the [[COMMITTEE]], the communication shall be approved for release.

  - Any employee who wishes to use social media or email to communicate with his or her customer or potential customers for a marketing purpose shall:
    - Advise the [[COMMITTEE]] of:
      - The method and type of communications that will be utilized.
      - The content of any marketing message or communication.
    - To deliver the message, the employee shall establish an approved social media channel for that purpose that is separate from the channel that the employee uses for personal communication.
    - No customer marketing communications shall be initiated until the employee receives the permission of the [[COMMITTEE]].

## Compliance

[[LEGAL_NAME]] will investigate and respond to all reports or violations of the Social Media Policy and other related policies. Violations of the company's Social Media Policy will result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination. Discipline or termination will be determined based on the nature of factors of any social networking post. [[SHORT_NAME]] reserves the right to take legal action, where necessary, against employees who engage in prohibited or unlawful conduct. Periodically, the [[COMMITTEE]] will occasionally review various social media services to identify any fictitious or non-sanctioned use
