### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to minimize the risk to [[SHORT_NAME]] information systems due to loss of equipment or data.

### Scope

The policy applies to the backup procedure of all information systems electronic data.

### Policy

This policy is a statement of the client's actual backup configuration. There is no shared prose to inherit. Populate each bullet from the client's environment and remove any that do not apply.

- Servers are being replicated to the DR site over [[DR_REPLICATION_LINK]] using [[BACKUP_SOFTWARE]] Backup & Replication software starting at [[DR_REPLICATION_START_TIME]] every night.
  - [[DR_RESTORE_POINT_COUNT]] restore points are kept offsite.
  - These replicas are ready to start as virtual machines on the DR server.
  - Transmission of replicas is encrypted using [[BACKUP_ENCRYPTION]]

- [[HOURLY_BACKUP_SERVERS]] are being backed up hourly to the local NAS device.
  - [[HOURLY_RESTORE_POINT_COUNT]] restore points are kept.
  - Images contain full server data needed to do a bare metal restore and individual file restore.
  - Backups are encrypted using [[BACKUP_ENCRYPTION]].

- [[DAILY_BACKUP_SERVERS]] are being backed up daily starting at [[DAILY_BACKUP_START_TIME]] to the local NAS device.
  - [[DAILY_RESTORE_POINT_COUNT]] restore points are kept.
  - Images contain full server data needed to do a bare metal restore and individual file restore.
  - Backups are encrypted using [[BACKUP_ENCRYPTION]].

- [[WEEKLY_BACKUP_ASSETS]] are being backed up weekly on [[WEEKLY_BACKUP_DAY]] to the local NAS device
  - [[WEEKLY_RESTORE_POINT_COUNT]] restore points are kept.
  - Images contain full server data needed to do a bare metal restore and individual file restore.
  - Backups are encrypted using [[BACKUP_ENCRYPTION]].

- All virtual machines are being backed up to the DR site after the nightly replicas have finished running.

- All local and offsite backups on the NAS devices are stored as immutable backups and cannot be altered (modified or deleted) for [[IMMUTABLE_RETENTION_DAYS]] days.

- The [[CLOUD_PRODUCTIVITY_SUITE]] environment data is being backed up daily to a NAS device with immutable backups at the main branch.

### Compliance

See Backup Guidelines in the ISPD for additional information and guidance.
