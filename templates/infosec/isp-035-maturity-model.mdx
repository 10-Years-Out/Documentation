## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the bank implements and maintains an Information and Cybersecurity maturity model.

## Scope

The policy applies to the adopted maturity model known as the [[MATURITY_MODEL_FULL]] Maturity Model and its related activities.

## Policy

- [[SHORT_NAME]] will implement a maturity model to track and assess the maturity of the information and cybersecurity program. For that purpose, the bank will adopt the [[MATURITY_MODEL]] Maturity Model.

- [[SHORT_NAME]] will use the [[MATURITY_MODEL]] Maturity Model at its discretion to establish reasonable and practical long-term goals in the ISTSP.

- [[SHORT_NAME]] will maintain/update the [[MATURITY_MODEL]] Maturity Model as needed and present it to the BOD for acceptance annually.

## Compliance

The changes to the maturity model will be reviewed and accepted by the [[COMMITTEE]].
