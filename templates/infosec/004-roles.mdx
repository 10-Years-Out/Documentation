## Board of Directors

- The BOD is responsible for delegating the creation and implementation of the information and cybersecurity program and this policy to the [[COMMITTEE]].

- The BOD must delegate the execution of this policy including assigning specific responsibility for its implementation to the [[COMMITTEE]] and reviewing reports from the Audit Committee so that the policy is enforced through proper controls.

- Approve [[SHORT_NAME]]'s written information and cybersecurity policy and program on an annual basis.

## [[COMMITTEE_FULL]]

The [[COMMITTEE]] is responsible for [[SHORT_NAME]]'s information and cybersecurity risks and shall exercise managerial oversight over the information and cybersecurity activities. To address its responsibility, the [[COMMITTEE]] will:

- Be responsible for implementing and supporting the information and cybersecurity policy.

- Handle information and cybersecurity as a strategic business concern, not just a technical issue.

- Be responsible for delegating the implementation to the [[COMMITTEE]] the information technology solutions in compliance with this policy and reporting any non-compliance or suspected security breaches to the CEO.

- Ensure that proper and effective supervision exists, and internal controls are implemented and maintained to insure the integrity of financial reporting systems.

- Delineate authority and responsibility, while retaining accountability for managing information and cybersecurity risk.

- Develop and maintain bank-wide personal computer ("PC") and network policies and procedures, including those related to contingency planning and disaster recovery.

- Ensure that disaster plans are formulated and tested for information security incidents.

- Examine segregation of duties and audit trails.

- Establish PC hardware and software standards, as well as approve new PC software packages.

- Oversee the purchase decision process and physical maintenance for all PC hardware and software.

- Evaluate PC and network maintenance agreements.

- Provide PC and network training programs.

- Violations of compliance will be handled by CEO on a case-by-case basis to determine whether the violation was intentional or unintentional and what appropriate action shall be taken.

## Employees

- Employees will be responsible for adhering to all policies and reporting any suspected problems to the [[COMMITTEE]].

- Each employee is accountable for protecting all information.

- Employees must become familiar with and apply the mandated practices dealing with proprietary information and computer systems security.
