## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure due diligence in service provider research and selection.

## Scope

This policy pertains to any outsourced operations or services.

## Policy

The [[COMMITTEE]] will determine if a service provider stores or transmits confidential or customer information. The [[COMMITTEE]] will determine if a service provider is significant enough to require annual reviews of performance and the relationship. Service provider contracts may include a signed Privacy Agreement with [[SHORT_NAME]], when possible, before doing work for [[SHORT_NAME]].

Additional service provider policy is available in the Service Provider Selection and Management Policy which is a separate document.

## Compliance

In the event a service provider is found to have violated this agreement, the [[COMMITTEE]] will address the issue and determine appropriate course of action.
