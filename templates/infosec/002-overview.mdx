The Board of Directors' (BOD) and chief executive officer's (CEO) intention for publishing an Information Security Policy and creating and maintaining an information security and cybersecurity program is not to impose restrictions that are contrary to [[LEGAL_NAME]] ([[SHORT_NAME]]) mission or goals but to protect the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of information and systems by establishing a process to identify threats, vulnerabilities, attacks, probabilities of occurrence, and outcomes. In addition, the BOD means to ensure that management and staff understand their responsibilities and has the knowledge and skills necessary to fulfill their duties without posing information and cybersecurity risks to the organization either knowingly or unknowingly.
