### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify and minimize the overall risk to [[SHORT_NAME]] information systems.

### Scope

The policy applies to all information systems and electronic data.

### Policy

- Information systems security risk assessments for critical information systems and critical software and applications will be performed periodically according FFIEC guidelines.

- All major enhancements, upgrades, and conversions associated with these systems or applications will be preceded by a risk assessment.

- The report resulting from this project will include a detailed description of the information security risks facing the bank and recommendations for mitigating the risks.

- The risk assessment will use a multidisciplinary approach to determine risks to [[SHORT_NAME]].

- The risk assessment will determine whether the risks are risks to confidentiality, availability, or integrity of [[SHORT_NAME]] information systems and information.

- The risk assessment will consider and evaluate the current cybersecurity threat landscape when assessing risk and ranking controls.

- The risk assessment will be reviewed and accepted by the BOD.

### Compliance

Refer to the FFIEC handbook for additional guidance on risk assessments.
