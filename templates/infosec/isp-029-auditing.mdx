## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the testing and verification of the status of [[SHORT_NAME]] information and cybersecurity program occurs and to ensure there is an established, comprehensive, and repeatable audit process/plan in place.

## Scope

The policy applies to all information systems, policies, and procedures at the bank as well as any external systems.

## Policy

The following are the steps that the bank takes in evaluating its IT audit universe and planning for its external 3rd party IT audits or proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.

### Understand the Organization

- Identify the strategies and business objectives of the institution.
  - This is accomplished through the formal IT strategic planning process.
- Recognize the risk profile for the institution.
  - This is accomplished through regular review and updates to the GLBA IT risk assessment and the IT risk appetite and tolerance statement.
- Assess how the organization structures its business operations.
- Comprehend the IT service support model.

### Define the IT Universe

- Analyze the business fundamentals
- Isolate significant applications that sustain the business operations and identify critical infrastructure for these applications. These are identified and defined in the following documents:
  - Applications overview
  - Network diagrams
  - Data flow diagrams
  - Information Security Program Documentation (ISPD)
- Understand all the roles of supporting technologies.
- Categorize major projects and initiatives
  - This is accomplished through the established IT project planning and IT budgeting process

### Perform Risk Assessment

- Evaluate business and IT processes to identify risk.
  - This is accomplished through each department's business impact analysis documents, including the IT department.
- Assess risks and rank audit subjects using IT risk factors and business risk factors.
  - This is accomplished through the GLBA IT risk assessment and IT Audit Risk Assessment.

### Formalize Audit Plan

- Choose audit subjects and group into distinct audit actions.
  - This will be accomplished through the IT Audit Risk Assessment & Audit Plan document, along with the IT audit scope.
  - Audit subjects/scope may change and shall be determined by factors including but not limited to: risk assessment findings, the occurrence of incidents or breaches, periods of significant change, current cybersecurity threat landscape and [[SHORT_NAME]]'s economic and financial conditions.
- Establish audit cycle and frequency.
  - 3rd Party IT audits of the information systems will be performed [[EXTERNAL_AUDIT_FREQUENCY]].
- Attach appropriate actions based on management requests or opportunities for consulting.
- Confirm and approve the audit plan/scope with the [[COMMITTEE]] and BOD.

## Compliance

The results of the IT audits will be reviewed by the IT department who will make remediation plans if necessary. These results and plans will be presented to and approved by the [[COMMITTEE]]. The [[COMMITTEE]] will oversee the overall IT audit planning process.
