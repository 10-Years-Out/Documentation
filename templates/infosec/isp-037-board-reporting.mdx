### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the BOD is involved in the information and cybersecurity program decisions.

### Scope

The policy applies to the information and cybersecurity program.

### Policy

- The [[COMMITTEE]] will periodically review information and cybersecurity program issues and status reports with the BOD.

- The [[COMMITTEE]] will present to the BOD policies, tests, assessments and findings as directed by policies that require BOD approval or acceptance.

- The [[COMMITTEE]] will present to the BOD an information and cybersecurity program project plan that includes the timing and costs of yearly expenditures.

### Compliance

The reports to the BOD along with decisions originating from the reports will be accepted and approved by the BOD.
