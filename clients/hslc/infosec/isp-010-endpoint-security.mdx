## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify the security controls in place to protect all endpoint devices on the network to reduce the risk of users accessing and using these devices for regular, daily tasks.

## Scope

The scope of this policy encompasses endpoint devices on the network (PCs, laptops, tablets, other mobile devices, etc.).

## Policy

### Endpoint Threat Protection

- Endpoint threat protection software shall be installed on all servers and workstations to proactively stop unauthorized activity by programs including but not limited to, malware, adware, spyware, key loggers, etc.

- The IT department shall be responsible for managing the endpoint threat protection system including, but not limited to, performing the following tasks:
  - Periodic review of devices in the console to ensure that the agent is installed and reporting into the console
  - Periodic review of the console for quarantined threats on devices to ensure no malicious activity has occurred and/or no repeating files and/or programs are being quarantined
  - Ensuring the agents are on the latest available, supported, and stable version
  - Ensuring the licensing is current and is renewed before the annual expiration date

- Endpoint threat protection agents shall be updated within 60 days of a new release.

- Endpoint threat protection algorithms shall be updated by the provider as necessary. After a device is initially scanned, the software then uses the algorithms to proactively block threats instead of periodically scanning.

- Employees are not permitted to edit or alter software settings and configurations without permission. These capabilities will be limited to authorized, administrative users using various hardware and software settings.

- The IT department will be responsible for investigation and reporting should a malware infection occur. See the Incident Response Policy & Plan for details on investigation and reporting procedures

### IDS/IPS

- Host based IDS/IPS agents shall be deployed to devices on the network.
  - Agents shall be configured to monitor for and detect potential security incidents and then stop the detected incident from continuing to execute

- Local server and workstation firewalls shall also be utilized and only allow open ports for traffic that is necessary to perform normal business functions.

- The IT department shall be responsible for managing the IDS/IPS solution and local firewall including, but not limited to, performing the following tasks:
  - Reviewing logs and alerts within the console on a periodic basis to determine if a pattern of malicious activity may be occurring or has occurred
  - Reviewing and responding to alerts generated by the system according to procedures outlined in the Incident Response Policy & Plan and the Information Security Program Documentation (ISPD)
  - Periodically performing a review of configured firewall rules to ensure only necessary traffic is allowed

### Patch Management & End-of-Life Management for Endpoint Devices

- See the Patch Management Policy for details on patch management processes and procedures.

- See the End of Life Management Policy for details on EOL processes and procedures.

## Compliance

The IT department will oversee the internal monitoring and review processes, including review reports, for all endpoint systems on the network. These reports will be presented to the ITSC identifying all low, medium and high risks relating to endpoint devices. Upon approval from the ITSC, the IT department will minimize these risks through a combination of shutting down unused services, closing ports, applying approved patches, updating software, and any other options available to secure the systems.
