## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish a change management program for relevant IT system changes.

## Scope

The scope of the IT change management process is limited to change implementations that will cause:

- A software or service to become unavailable or degraded during service hours.
- The functionality of a software or service to change.
- Security to be lessened.
  - Allowing additional traffic or access through the perimeter security
- Changes to the network access documentation or significant change to the configuration documentation.

Other IT changes shall not require formal change management. Instead, they shall be tracked as standard IT activities or when warranted as projects.

## Policy

### General

A change management process shall be utilized for items defined in the scope. The IT department shall be responsible for the execution of the process and maintenance of the procedures. Department heads and data owners shall participate in the process when the changes affect their operational functionality or when they are the requestor of the change.

- The IT department shall document the changes in the appropriate documentation.
- The IT department shall manage changes for users, groups, roles, data stores, and applications through the enrollment/IAM process.

### Approval

- Most changes shall be accounted for and approved through the annual IT project plan process, which is managed by the IT department. Normally, this process shall have documented approval from a member of the ITSC before changes are implemented into production.
- The IT department shall discuss perimeter/firewall Access Control List changes verbally. Then they shall present the changes quarterly to the ITSC for approval.

### Testing

- The IT department shall test changes to determine that the changes had the desired results.
  - If the change did not achieve the desired results, the IT department shall remediate the issues.

### Emergency Changes

- Emergency changes that fall into the scope of this policy shall receive the change management process retroactively. The change management process shall not inhibit emergency changes because the goal of technology is to forward the business objectives of HSLC.

## Compliance

The ITSC will ensure that the controls outlined in this policy are evaluated and reviewed according to the Auditing Policy.
