### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish a standard to identify safeguards in place to protect against unauthorized internal or external network access.

### Scope

The scope of this policy includes all network access methods, including: wireless (Wi-Fi), Ethernet, dedicated data lines, remote dial-up access, extranets, and the Internet.

### Policy

- The ITSC shall approve any new protocols or network access methods for applications, employees, and service providers before introduction to or implementation on the HSLC network. Refer to: LAN-WAN Network and Data Flow diagrams in the ISPD.

- Hardware and/or software shall be in place to inspect and block unauthorized traffic and network activities.

- Hardware and/or software settings and configurations that allow network access shall be reviewed periodically.

### Compliance

The IT department should include network access related hardware and/or software in monitoring, alerting, auditing and change management activities in accordance with the following policies: Monitoring & Alerting Policy, Auditing Policy, and Change Management Policy.
