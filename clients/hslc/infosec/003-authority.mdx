Information security is the responsibility of everyone at HSLC as well as HSLC service providers and contractors.

The customers of HSLC have entrusted the employees of HSLC with protecting the confidentiality and security of their information. We are committed to fulfilling these responsibilities.

The BOD and IT Steering Committee (ITSC) will ensure that HSLC is in compliance with all laws, regulations and requirements pertaining to information and cybersecurity by overseeing risk assessment, development of policies, standards and procedures, testing and security reporting processes. This will be accomplished with the assistance of outside vendors who have sufficient knowledge, background, and training in the area of information and cybersecurity.

The ITSC will enforce its Information Security Policy and clearly communicate responsibilities and accountability for complying with these requirements.
