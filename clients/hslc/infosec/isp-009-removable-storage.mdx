## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify the safeguards in place to restrict and control access to and use of removable storage devices and media.

## Scope

The scope of this policy encompasses restricting and controlling the ability to utilize removable storage methods at the user and workstation level, insuring controls exist to provide safe use of removable storage, and providing a structure to track approved users and devices.

## Policy

- The bank will disable the capability to use removable storage using Group Policy Objects (GPO) which will restrict unauthorized users from using those features on their workstation.

- Authorized users will be approved by the ITSC on the basis that their job role has a demonstrated need for the use of removable storage features. Authorized users should be documented and tracked.

- The bank will issue approved USB devices to the authorized users and should track the issuance of the devices.

- The USB devices will be encrypted using BitLocker and have a password length of 10 characters.

- Authorized users will abide by all policies and sign documentation acknowledging their understanding of the responsibility that accompanies the use of removable storage.

## Compliance

The ITSC will insure that the controls outlined in this policy are evaluated and audited periodically including evaluating the job roles that are approved for use.
