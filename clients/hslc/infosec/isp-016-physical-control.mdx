### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to identify the safeguards in place to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information system related items.

### Scope

This policy applies to access to hardware, software, and storage media.

### Policy

- All host systems, software, and storage media shall be located in a restricted area.

- All workstations and laptops shall be locked when unattended and during non-business hours unless they are being utilized by authorized personnel.

- All portable devices (i.e. laptops, cell phones, removable storage) shall have an extra layer of security to reduce the risk of compromising confidential information.

- All confidential, proprietary, or privileged information transmitting in and out of the bank network shall be encrypted with a recommended key length of 2048 bits except for any exemption made by the ITSC that appear in the appropriate exceptions log.

#### Computer Room

- Access to the computer room is limited to authorized personnel through the use of a door key lock. The ITSC will determine authorized personnel.

  - Visitors receiving clearance must sign the Visitors log
