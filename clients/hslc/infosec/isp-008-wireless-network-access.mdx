### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish a standard to design, implement, and identify safeguards in place to protect against unauthorized internal or external wireless network access.

### Scope

The scope of this policy includes wireless network implementation and access methods.

### Policy

- The ITSC shall establish wireless networks that are purpose driven and follow the principle of least privilege.

- The IT department shall be responsible for diagramming, implementing, securing, monitoring, and maintaining the wireless networks.

- The IT department must have the permission of the ITSC and a written description of the purpose and list expected users before implementing any wireless network.

- The wireless networks shall be monitored by the IT department

- The ITSC shall establish an implementation/ hardening standard for wireless networks based on the purpose.
  - The networks shall be password protected with a minimum character limit of 12.
  - The networks shall use a minimum encryption of AES-256 with wpa2-psk.
  - The wireless networks shall be configured as one network allowing internet only and one network allowing LAN access.
  - Access by employees on any networks shall abide by the enrollment and exit process.
  - Refer to the SonicWall Wireless AP ISPD setup doc for details on configuration information.

- Changes to the wireless networks or their supporting infrastructure shall abide by the established change management process.

- Supervisors of the departments that are requesting or granting the access shall be responsible for executing the appropriate enrollment and exit processes and training.

- Management should consider establishing networks for the following purposes including but not limited to:
  - Corporate network that would allow access to bank LAN, servers and/or applications.
    - This network shall have the strictest enrollment process.
  - Internet only network that has full access to the internet as a business device, but does not require any access to the LAN.
    - This should be separated from the production network using hardware and/or software controls.
  - Vendor/Auditor network for use by approved providers.
    - This should be separated from the production network using hardware or software controls.
  - Guest device network could be provided for bank guests that may be conducting business at the bank and need a stable connection.
    - This should be separated from the production network using hardware or software controls.

- Hardware and/or software settings and configurations that allow network access shall be reviewed periodically.

- Hardware and/or software shall be in place to inspect and block unauthorized traffic and network activities.

### Compliance

The IT department shall include wireless network access related hardware and/or software in monitoring, alerting, auditing and change management activities in accordance with the following policies: Monitoring & Alerting Policy, Auditing Policy, and Change Management Policy.
