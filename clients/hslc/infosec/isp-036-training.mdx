## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the appropriate training program is implemented and maintained for all employees of the bank with the goal of making operations more secure.

## Scope

The policy applies to all bank employees regarding information and cybersecurity topics.

## Policy

- The ITSC shall oversee the implementation and management of an information and cybersecurity training program. The program should have the following characteristics:
  - Be repeatable, measurable and trackable.

  - Attempt to provide training on general topics and topics that are specific to the bank.

  - Strive to include material that is commensurate with the duties performed by the employees.

  - Be capable of evolving to meet the needs of the bank.

  - Be a scheduled, ongoing process that strengthens the security awareness of all employees.

  - Nurture a security aware culture.

- Training modules provided to users shall include the topics of spam, phishing, spear phishing, malware, social engineering, and any other topics deemed necessary and beneficial to furthering the education and security awareness of bank employees. Specific modules are based off of industry standards and events and the scope of the bank's information and cybersecurity program.

- Any employee who fails a training course will have one attempt to retake the course. If a second failure should occur, the employee will be required to meet directly with their supervisor to determine the best course of action for the employee to be properly educated on the training material.
  - If an employee fails a social engineering training course, they will be required to meet with their supervisor directly to discuss the appropriate actions that need to be taken in the specific social engineering situation.

- Along with reporting social engineering/phishing emails during training, employees are required to report real social engineering/phishing attempts immediately to their direct supervisor.

- New employees shall complete the KnowBe4 onboarding course within their probationary period.

- Employees shall be expected to put forth an earnest effort to participate in, benefit from, and put into practice the information presented in the training program.

## Compliance

The ITSC shall review progress reports for the program during ITSC meetings. Relevant information shall be reported to the BOD. The proactive monitoring and maintenance processes shall assess relevant aspects of the program for review by the ITSC.
