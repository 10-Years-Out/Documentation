## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish parameters for the appropriate disposal of all data.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and third parties and is inclusive of any forms of electronic or tangible media that may contain confidential data.

## Policy

- The ITSC shall establish and oversee a process for managing and insuring that when hardware or media containing confidential information is no longer required, and when legal or regulatory requirements for its retention no longer apply, it shall be destroyed according to approved methods. The IT department shall be responsible for executing processes that enforce this policy.

  - Disposal in trashcans or recycling bins is prohibited.

  - All confidential information must be shredded if in paper form.

  - Confidential information resident on disks, magnetic tapes, hard drives (installed or portable), mass storage devices (USB), mobile devices (personal & bank owned), and other computer magnetic storage media must be destroyed via device wiping, multiple reformatting, or degaussing if the media is to be reused by the data owner or the bank.

  - Simple "deletion" or "erasure" of confidential information is not sufficient because the information is still recoverable. If the media will not be used again, the information may be definitively destroyed with scissors, punching, drilling, shredding, or any other safe method that renders the device/data irrecoverable.

  - The process shall include evaluating the device for confidential information and allowing the data owner to confirm that any discovered information is safe for removal.

  - The process shall include mechanisms that promote tracking and verification of the disposal and auditing.

- Data disposal or destruction as a service by a third party or for data held or handled by a third party are subject to the Service Provider Management Policy, the terms of the agreement with the third party, and should include certification.

- All employees are trained on the appropriate handling of information through the following policies:

  - Privacy Policy, Personnel Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy

## Compliance

Auditing and reporting shall be performed through the Internal proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.
