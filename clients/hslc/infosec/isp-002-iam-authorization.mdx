### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to establish a standard to identify and restrict access to all system resources to the minimum required for carrying out legitimate and approved activities on HSL information systems, and to define how the rights will be administered.

### Scope

The scope of this policy includes all the user account rights assignment of all HSL information systems and software and data.

### Policy

- Accounts and devices shall be given the minimum authorization necessary to fulfill the user's job function and will be enforced through group policy on the network and settings on core and third-party applications.

- Where possible, systems shall integrate access authorization with the Windows domain, either through direct LDAP or RADIUS support.

- The formal access rights administration for users will consist of:

  - An enrollment process to add new users to the system based on the new users role.
  - Authorization process to add, delete, or modify authorized user access to network devices, operating systems, applications, folders, files, and specific type of information.
    - For procedures, forms, and documentation, reference:
      - Enrollment Tracking Updates document in ISPD
      - User Access Request Form
      - Identity and Access Management (IAM) Control Documentation
  - An authentication process to identify the user during subsequent activities.
  - A monitoring process.
    - For reference:
      - HSL Kenton System & Network Monitoring Overview
  - An auditing process consisting of proactive monitoring and maintenance processes of roles, users, groups, folders, and permissions across the environment.
    - For procedures, scope, and documentation, reference:
      - Quarterly IAM Review

### Compliance

It will be the responsibility of the ITSC to inform the IT department when access rights need to be added, deleted, or modified. The ITSC will oversee access rights granted. All users are required to agree and follow the Acceptable Use Policy.
