### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that information and cybersecurity program policies are updated.

### Scope

The policy applies to all information and cybersecurity program policies.

### Policy

- HSLC will update the information and cybersecurity program policies periodically as the cybersecurity threat landscape changes, new information and or technology becomes available.

- HSLC will log significant changes to the policies.

### Compliance

The changes will be reviewed and accepted by the ITSC.
