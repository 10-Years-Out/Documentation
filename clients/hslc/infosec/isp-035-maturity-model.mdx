## Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the bank implements and maintains an Information and Cybersecurity maturity model.

## Scope

The policy applies to the adopted maturity model known as the Cyber Risk Institute (CRI) Profile Maturity Model and its related activities.

## Policy

- HSLC will implement a maturity model to track and assess the maturity of the information and cybersecurity program. For that purpose, the bank will adopt the CRI Profile Maturity Model.

- HSLC will use the CRI Profile Maturity Model at its discretion to establish reasonable and practical long-term goals in the ISTSP.

- HSLC will maintain/update the CRI Profile Maturity Model as needed and present it to the BOD for acceptance annually.

## Compliance

The changes to the maturity model will be reviewed and accepted by the ITSC.
