### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to minimize the risk to HSLC information systems due to loss of equipment or data.

### Scope

The policy applies to the backup procedure of all information systems electronic data.

### Policy

- Servers are being replicated to the DR site over a fiber line using Veeam Backup & Replication software starting at 11:00 PM every night.
  - 28 restore points are kept offsite.
  - These replicas are ready to start as virtual machines on the DR server.
  - Transmission of replicas is encrypted using AES-256

- FILESERVER, LASERPRO, APPSERVER-H2, and APPSERVER-H1 are being backed up hourly to the local NAS device.
  - 275 restore points are kept.
  - Images contain full server data needed to do a bare metal restore and individual file restore.
  - Backups are encrypted using AES-256.

- All other virtual servers and the LIQUIDFILES and VCENTER appliances are being backed up daily starting at 6:00 PM to the local NAS device.
  - 30 restore points are kept.
  - Images contain full server data needed to do a bare metal restore and individual file restore.
  - Backups are encrypted using AES-256.

- All other virtual appliances are being backed up weekly on Saturdays to the local NAS device
  - 8 restore points are kept.
  - Images contain full server data needed to do a bare metal restore and individual file restore.
  - Backups are encrypted using AES-256.

- All virtual machines are being backed up to the DR site after the nightly replicas have finished running.

- All local and offsite backups on the NAS devices are stored as immutable backups and cannot be altered (modified or deleted) for 30 days.

- The Microsoft 365 environment data is being backed up daily to a NAS device with immutable backups at the main branch.
