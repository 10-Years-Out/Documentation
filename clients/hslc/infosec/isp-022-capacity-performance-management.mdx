### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to empower the ITSC to ensure the bank's efforts to manage and report capacity and/or performance in order to protect the availability of HSLC's information systems.

### Scope

This policy applies to all servers existing on the HLSC network and the roles involved in monitoring and managing the system.

### Policy

The ITSC shall direct IT resources to collect information and report capacity/performance for servers. The ITSC may accomplish this through either in-house resources or through a third party service. Tools, software, and hardware may include the following:

- Configurable alerts and reports that are native to existing software and hardware.

- Software and/or hardware designed and implemented specifically to detect and alert on network performance.

- The frequency of reporting shall be no less than 30 days, which may be accomplished through the bank's proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.

- The reports will be reviewed in summary by the ITSC during ITSC meetings.

- The ITSC will provide summary reports to the BOD periodically.

#### Capacity Planning and Management

- The ITSC shall consider server capacity/performance in the creation of all long-term strategic plans and annual project plans.
  - The ITSC will consider how to efficiently retire and remove data.
  - The ITSC will project future data storage needs and server capacity requirements and use the projections in planning.
  - The bank will consider how new services, systems, customers and employees will affect data storage needs and overhead on servers.

- If capacity/performance concerns arise during a project plan year, the situation will be evaluated and added to the current year's project plan if necessary.

- The following resources shall be available to allocate to servers at any given time in the virtual environment.
  - At least 30% of free, available disk space for servers.
  - At least 25% of free, available CPU allocation for servers.
  - At least 25% of free, available RAM allocation for servers.

#### Reporting Expectations and Current Configurations

- For an overview of monitoring/reporting capabilities and guidelines, see the System and Network Monitoring document.

- The following thresholds shall be set for server resources and alerts generated if the thresholds are crossed
  - Any server CPU usage that exceeds 80% for 60 minutes.
  - Any server RAM usage that exceeds 90% for 60 minutes.
  - Any server disk drive the falls below 10% of free, available disk space

- For specific performance/capacity configurations, see the ISPD.

### Compliance

- The ITSC is responsible for overseeing the implementation of reporting/monitoring capabilities and reviewing reports to determine if capacity is appropriately managed. Reports will be reviewed through existing normal processes such as ITSC meetings, and the bank's proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.

- If warranted the by results of the reports, the ITSC shall direct IT resources to investigate and remediate any concerning issues through the bank's proactive monitoring and maintenance processes.
