### Intent

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the bank implements and maintains an Information Security and Technology Strategic Plan (ISTSP).

### Scope

The policy applies to all information and cybersecurity program aspects and the long term planning for the program.

### Policy

- HSLC will create an Information Security and Technology Strategic Plan that will outline the high-level strategic goals for all information systems at the Home Savings and Loan Company of Kenton, Ohio (HSL). It is the intention of HSL that IT related initiatives support the business objectives and overall strategic plans of the bank. The plan should cover a forward looking period of three to five years.
  - The plan should contain conceptual goals that serve the interests of our customers, community, and staff and infrastructure goals that support those groups while increasing the security and efficiency of the bank.
  - The plan should contain goals that move the bank forward in its maturity model,

- HSLC will maintain/update the ISTSP as needed and present it to the BOD for acceptance annually.

### Compliance

The changes will be reviewed and accepted by the ITSC.
