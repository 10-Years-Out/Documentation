---
title: "Policy documentation"
description: "Information security policy templates and the per-client copies derived from them"
---

This site holds the information security policy templates CBC maintains and the full policy set adopted by each client bank.

Use **Templates** for the tokenized source text of each policy. Placeholders are written as `[[TOKEN]]` and stand in for anything that differs between banks: names, committees, vendors, products, and thresholds.

Use the client dropdown for that bank's adopted copy. **HSLC** is the first client on the site.

Client policy files are standalone. Each one contains its own full text and does not import or inherit from a template.
