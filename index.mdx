<Warning>
  The values in this test branch are demonstration data. Do not give these records to a client or auditor as evidence of a completed implementation.
</Warning>

## How the documents stay current

Client-specific values are stored once in the `variables` section of `docs.json`. The overview, implementation standard, and audit record use the same global references. When a switch is replaced, update its variables and review the three documents before publication.
