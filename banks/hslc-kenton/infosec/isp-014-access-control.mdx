## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for granting, reviewing, and revoking access to information systems, data, and facilities. It is intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized use while allowing employees and service providers to perform assigned duties. Access decisions are based on job function and the principle of least privilege.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and other third parties who are granted access to the institution's systems or data. It covers user accounts, privileged accounts, service accounts, and remote access channels. Physical access to restricted computing areas is included to the extent it is used to protect information assets.

## 3. Policy Statement

Access to systems and data is provisioned only when a documented business need exists and an authorized approver has approved the request. Accounts are unique to a person or a registered service identity. Shared credentials are prohibited except where a system cannot support individual accounts and compensating controls are in place.

### 3.1 Account provisioning and authentication

New access is requested through the institution's access request process and is approved by the employee's manager and the system owner. Authentication uses unique user identifiers and passwords that meet the institution's complexity and rotation standards. Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access and for administrative interfaces.

### 3.2 Privileged access review

Privileged access assignments are reviewed monthly so elevated rights do not linger after a role change. The Information Security Officer, or a designated reviewer, confirms that each privileged account remains necessary, correctly scoped, and assigned to an active employee or service identity. Accounts that fail this review are disabled pending remediation.

### 3.3 Access termination

When employment or a contractor engagement ends, or when job duties change, access is revoked or adjusted on the same business day that the change takes effect. System owners confirm that terminated users cannot authenticate to production systems. Residual access identified after termination is treated as a control exception and is documented.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

The Information Security Officer maintains this policy, oversees access reviews, and reports exceptions to management. System owners approve access to the systems they manage and complete periodic recertification. Managers request and attest to access for their staff. All users protect credentials, report suspected misuse, and use only the access assigned to them.

## 5. Enforcement

Failure to follow this policy may result in revocation of access, disciplinary action, or termination of a vendor relationship, consistent with the institution's personnel and vendor management procedures. Suspected unauthorized access is reported to the Information Security Officer and is investigated. Material control failures are escalated to the designated oversight committee.

## 6. Review and Approval

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or sooner if systems, regulations, or organizational structure change in a way that affects access control. Proposed revisions are presented to the designated oversight committee and, where required, to the board of directors. The current approved version is the version published in this documentation site.
