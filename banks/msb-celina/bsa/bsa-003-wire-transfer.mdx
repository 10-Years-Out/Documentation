## 1. Purpose

This policy sets requirements for originating, verifying, and recording domestic and international wire transfers. It is designed to prevent unauthorized funds movement and to support Bank Secrecy Act and funds-transfer recordkeeping obligations. Staff who initiate or approve wires are expected to follow these controls on every transaction.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all wire transfers originated, received, or amended through the institution, including customer-requested wires and internal settlement transfers. It covers staff in operations, retail, and treasury functions who handle wire instructions. Automated clearing house transactions and internal book transfers that are not processed as wires are outside the scope of this policy.

## 3. Policy Statement

Wire transfers are processed only from authenticated requests that contain complete beneficiary and originator information. The institution does not act on wire instructions received solely by unverified email. Exceptions require documented approval from an authorized officer before the wire is released.

### 3.1 Wire initiation

A wire is initiated only after the requesting party is identified using the institution's customer or employee authentication procedures. Required fields include originator name, account number, beneficiary name, beneficiary account, receiving bank, and amount. Incomplete requests are returned to the requester and are not queued for release.

### 3.2 Callback and verification

Before a wire is released, operations staff verify the request using an independent callback or another out-of-band method listed in the wire procedures. Verification confirms the amount, beneficiary, and receiving institution against the authenticated request. Failed or incomplete verification stops the wire until the discrepancy is resolved.

### 3.3 Recordkeeping

The institution retains wire requests, verification evidence, and transmittal records for at least five years, or longer if a longer period is required by law or by the primary regulator. Records identify the employee who initiated the wire and the employee who released it. These records are available to audit, compliance, and law enforcement upon proper request.

### 3.4 Dual authorization for high-value wires

Wires of $25,000 or more require dual authorization before release. A second authorized employee, who did not originate the request, reviews the amount, beneficiary, and verification evidence and records an independent approval. A single employee may not both enter and release a wire at or above this threshold, including when acting in a supervisory capacity.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

The Bank Secrecy Act Officer oversees wire-related BSA controls and exception reporting. Operations supervisors assign dual-control duties and confirm that staff who release wires are trained. Front-line staff collect complete wire instructions and do not coach customers to circumvent verification. Internal audit tests adherence to this policy on a periodic basis.

## 5. Enforcement

Wires processed outside this policy are reported as exceptions to the Bank Secrecy Act Officer and to the designated oversight committee. Employees who willfully bypass verification or dual-control requirements are subject to disciplinary action under the institution's personnel policy. Repeated operational errors trigger retraining and, if uncorrected, restriction of wire privileges.

## 6. Review and Approval

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or after a material change in wire systems, correspondent relationships, or applicable BSA requirements. Revisions are submitted to the designated oversight committee and, where required, to the board of directors. The published version in this documentation site is the version in effect.
