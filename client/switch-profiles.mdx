## Editing rules

- Keep `switch-reference` stable when you replace a physical switch.
- Use plain, printable values for monitoring and MFA status.
- Do not enter passwords, private keys, or other secrets.
- Review the commands and configuration record after Mintlify rebuilds the branch.
